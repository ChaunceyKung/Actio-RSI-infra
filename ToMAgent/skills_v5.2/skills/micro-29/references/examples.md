# micro-29 Examples and Boundaries

## Decision Variable
the social display rule governing expression

## Route Signal
Use this micro unit when a character's outward emotion is strategically shaped by a display rule tied to politeness, role, audience, or situation.

## Hard Boundary
- This unit is about rule-governed expression control, not all hidden emotion.
- The decisive evidence must include a display norm or social expectation.
- The answer must NAME the social display rule driving the outward expression; the inner feeling may be referenced as context but is not the answer itself (the bare inner feeling is the detect-the-mismatch capability, micro-28).
- Do not infer deception automatically; many display rules are polite, protective, professional, or ceremonial rather than dishonest.

## Shortcut To Avoid
- Do not assume the displayed emotion is the true emotion just because it is visible.
- Do not label every smile as happiness; check whether the social setting rewards that display.
- Do not give the bare inner feeling when the question asks WHY the person manages the expression - that bare-feeling answer is the detect-the-mismatch capability (micro-28), not this unit.
- Do not over-moralize polite masking as lying unless truthfulness or intent to mislead is the main issue.

## Common Failure Modes
- Answering with the bare inner feeling and omitting the social display rule the question is really asking for.
- Ignoring the audience and treating the expression as purely personal.
- Confusing rule-governed display control with actually changing the feeling (emotion regulation).
- Confusing emotional disguise with an atypical emotion.
- Treating culturally or role-specific display expectations as universal without using scene evidence.

## Minimal Pair
- Case: Chen Fei wins a card game but keeps a calm face; asked WHY he appears calm, the answer is 'to avoid upsetting his friend.' | Why: The question targets the social reason for the managed display - a do-not-gloat politeness rule - which is exactly this unit.
- Case: Same Chen Fei scene, but asked 'What are Chen Fei's real feelings?' the answer is 'happy.' | Why: This routes to the detect-the-mismatch capability (micro-28): it asks only for the bare inner feeling, with no social rule needed.

## Boundary Stress Test
- Zhao Bo, a club president, hides pain after falling to keep a confident face: the leadership role is the display rule, so this unit applies when the question asks why he looks confident.
- If a contestant smiles after losing, check whether sportsmanship or public-facing expectations govern the expression rather than genuine happiness - if so, this unit applies.
- If a slighted boy tells himself he is a 'secret weapon' and turns to the next game, route to the emotion-regulation capability (micro-33): he changes the feeling, he does not just control a display.
- If someone laughs at bad news because of a genuinely unusual appraisal, route to the atypical-emotion capability (micro-27) unless a display rule explains the laugh.
- If someone apologizes and feels guilt, route to the moral-emotion capability (micro-30) when responsibility appraisal is the central variable, even if they also control their face.

## Generalization Note
Display rules vary by culture, role, relationship, and situation, but the operational pattern is stable: infer the felt emotion, identify the social expectation, and explain the controlled outward display - while the inner feeling itself stays unchanged.
